HOW SCENARIOS SAY "MUST" AND "MUST NOT": the user goal sets the outcome (Must achieve, or the rejected option for a customer
who should be turned down); the expectation on the right (a tool call with its inputs, or an agent response) says what must
happen; anything that must NOT happen is a yes/no question from + Add expectations, which passes only on "Yes". Those
questions are saved to the app (names must be unique), so later scenarios just tick them.
